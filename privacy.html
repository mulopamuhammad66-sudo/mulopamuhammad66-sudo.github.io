Before you upload: Beacon Stream is a public video-sharing app. Videos, captions, profile information and community interactions can be visible to other people. Do not upload private information you do not want others to see.

01 / Who runs Beacon Stream

Beacon Stream is an early-access Android video-sharing project operated by Muhammad Mulopa. This policy covers the app and explains its current account, video and community features.

For privacy questions or account and content deletion requests, contact mulopamuhammad66@gmail.com.

02 / Information collected

  • Account information: an email address, account identifier, username and display name. Email registration uses a password handled by the authentication provider.
  • Google sign-in, when enabled: Google may provide your Google account identifier, email address, name and basic profile information such as a profile picture, depending on the permissions granted. A profile picture is not currently displayed by the app. Beacon Stream does not receive your Google password.
  • Content you submit: selected video files, original file names, captions, comments and report descriptions, together with account identifiers and timestamps.
  • Community activity: likes and following relationships used to provide the feed and community features.
  • Technical information: app requests necessarily send connection information, such as an IP address, to hosting and authentication services. These providers may process security and operational logs.
  • Support messages: information you choose to include when contacting the operator.

The app uploads the video file you choose; it does not need access to your entire media library to publish that file. Files may contain embedded metadata, so review them before sharing.

03 / How information is used

Information is used to create and authenticate accounts, keep you signed in, associate content with its creator, display public videos and comments, process likes and follows, show a following feed, receive reports, respond to support requests and maintain the service.

Google account information is used for sign-in, account identification and basic profile creation. The current sign-in flow does not request access to Gmail messages, Google Drive files, contacts or calendars. Google sign-in availability depends on the app version and the project's OAuth configuration.

The reviewed app source does not include advertising or a third-party advertising analytics SDK. Google account data is not used by this implementation for targeted advertising.

04 / What is public

Profiles, usernames, display names, bios, uploaded videos, captions, comments, likes and following relationships are publicly readable in the current service design. Video files are stored in a public media bucket and may be accessible to anyone who has their URL, including people without an account.

Your sign-in email is not intentionally published in the public profile or video feed. Report descriptions are not part of the public feed; they are associated with the reporting account and may be accessed by the operator to address reports.

Other people can copy, download or reshare public content. Removing content from the service cannot guarantee removal of copies made by others.

05 / Services that process information

Supabase provides authentication, database storage and uploaded-video storage. Account information, community records and uploaded files are processed through the project's Supabase services.

Google provides optional Google authentication. When you choose Google sign-in, Google processes the sign-in request under its own privacy terms and shares the approved account information with the app's authentication service.

Website hosting processes requests when you visit these public information pages. This website does not ask you to create an account, upload videos or enter a password.

The operator and service providers may access information as needed to operate the service, address security issues or meet applicable legal obligations. Processing may occur outside your country, depending on provider infrastructure and project configuration.

06 / Storage, security & retention

The app uses authenticated sessions and database access rules to restrict account changes and uploads. Public-content access is intentional. No online service can guarantee absolute security.

Sessions are persisted on your device by the authentication library. Signing out ends the app's active session on that device; it does not delete your account or previously published content.

Account and community records are retained while needed to operate the account and service, unless removed following a verified request or for another legitimate reason. There is currently no published automatic expiry period. Provider logs, backups or records needed for security or legal reasons may remain for their applicable retention periods.

07 / Your choices & deletion requests

You can choose whether to register, which videos to upload, and whether to use email or Google sign-in where available. The app lets you remove your own likes and unfollow accounts.

The current app does not provide a complete self-service account-deletion screen. To request access, correction, account deletion or removal of your videos and associated information, email mulopamuhammad66@gmail.com with the subject Beacon Stream privacy request. Where possible, write from the email connected to your account and identify the account or content concerned. Never send a password, authentication code or identity document unless an appropriate secure verification method has been agreed.

The operator may need to verify account ownership before acting. A deletion request should cover both database records and uploaded media files. Some records may need to be retained for legitimate security or legal reasons, and third-party copies of public content may remain.

You can also remove the app's Google connection through your Google Account's third-party connections settings. Revoking Google access does not itself delete the Beacon Stream account or content. Depending on your location, applicable law may provide additional privacy rights.

08 / Children & sensitive information

Beacon Stream is not intended for children under 13, or below any higher minimum age required by applicable law. A parent or guardian who believes a child has provided personal information should contact the operator to request review and removal.

Do not publish passwords, financial details, private contact information or other sensitive information about yourself or another person. Only share content you have the right to publish.

09 / Changes & contact

This policy may be updated as the app develops. The effective date at the top identifies the current version. Significant changes to data use should be explained before they take effect.
